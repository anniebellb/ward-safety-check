# Ward Safety Check: what's in it

A hospital ward with three patient bays and a lab prep area. Eight things need fixing and
two are look-alikes. Every option has feedback that says why.

| Where | Item | What's wrong | The fix the learner does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bay 1 | Full red bag | The bag lining the medical waste container is full | Ties it with one knot in the middle, double-bags it, carries it to the medical waste receptacle |
| Bay 1 | Gloves and hand rub | Nothing (look-alike): clean supplies belong on the supply cart | — |
| Bay 2 | Sharps container on the cart | At the ¾ line, and on the clean supply cart | Seals it, carries it to the medical waste pickup area |
| Bay 2 | Broken lab tube | Broken glass on the floor beside the bed | Fetches a plastic dust pan and hand broom, sweeps it up, puts it in the lab's sharps container |
| Bay 3 | Overfilled sharps container | Past the ¾ line | Seals it, carries it to the medical waste pickup area; a new one appears |
| Lab Prep Area | Refrigerator | Holds blood samples, no label | Adds a biohazard label and a "Lab Specimens Only" sign |
| Lab Prep Area | Centrifuge | Could be contaminated, no label | Adds a biohazard label |
| Lab Prep Area | Transport container | Carries blood samples, no label | Adds a biohazard label |
| Lab Prep Area | Medical waste container | No label | Adds a biohazard label |
| Lab Prep Area | Clean supply cabinet | Nothing (look-alike): clean storage doesn't need a label | — |

The end screen ("Floor Is Safe") sums it up in four lines: red bags, sharps containers,
broken glass and biohazard labels.

This is general safety practice written for learning, not any organisation's own rules.
